Calorie Calculator

Estimate daily calorie requirements.

How to use

  1. Enter sex, age, height and weight.
  2. Pick your activity level and goal.
  3. Read your BMR, maintenance calories and daily target.

Example

A 30-year-old man, 175 cm, 72 kg, lightly active: BMR ≈ 1,669 kcal, maintenance ≈ 2,295 kcal.

Helpful information

Which formula is used?

Mifflin-St Jeor: 10×kg + 6.25×cm − 5×age, then +5 for men or −161 for women, times an activity factor.

How fast should I lose weight?

The "lose" goal subtracts 500 kcal a day, roughly 0.5 kg a week. Slower is fine too. See a doctor or dietitian for medical conditions.
