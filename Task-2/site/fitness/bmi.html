BMI Calculator

Check BMI and understand your range.

How to use

  1. Pick metric or feet/inches.
  2. Enter height and weight.
  3. Choose WHO or Asian ranges and read your category.

Example

170 cm and 65 kg: BMI = 65 / 1.7² = 22.5, Normal in both WHO and Asian ranges.

Helpful information

WHO or Asian ranges?

WHO: overweight starts at 25. For South Asian adults, the WHO Asia-Pacific guidance uses 23, because health risks rise at lower BMI. Ask your doctor which applies to you.

Is BMI reliable?

It is a screening number. It does not separate muscle from fat and is not meant for children, pregnancy or athletes.
