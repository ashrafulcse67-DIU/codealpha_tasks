How to use

  1. Enter the original price and the discount %.
  2. Add a second discount or VAT only if you need them.
  3. Read the final price and how much you save.

Helpful information

Are two discounts added together?

No. The second discount applies to the already reduced price, so 20% then 10% is 28% in total, not 30%.

Which VAT rate should I use?

Check the rate that applies to your product. 15% is the standard VAT rate in Bangladesh, but some items differ.
