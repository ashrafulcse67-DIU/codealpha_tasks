SSC/HSC GPA

Enter marks, get grades and your board GPA.

Edit names or add and remove subjects to match your own group. Enter total marks out of 100.

How to use

  1. Choose SSC or HSC to load a typical subject list, then edit names or add and remove rows.
  2. Enter marks out of 100 for each subject. Combine paper 1 and paper 2 into one mark out of 100.
  3. Tick the 4th subject. Read your GPA and grade.

Example

Marks of 85, 72, 66, 91, 58, 77 and a 4th subject at 80 (A+). Compulsory points: 5+4+3.5+5+3+4 = 24.5. Bonus 5 − 2 = 3. GPA = 27.5 / 6 = 4.58.

Helpful information

Marks to grade table?

80–100 A+ (5.00), 70–79 A (4.00), 60–69 A- (3.50), 50–59 B (3.00), 40–49 C (2.00), 33–39 D (1.00), below 33 F (0.00).

Is this my official result?

No. Official results come from your education board. Use this to check or plan. Some subjects have separate pass marks for each part, which this page does not check.
