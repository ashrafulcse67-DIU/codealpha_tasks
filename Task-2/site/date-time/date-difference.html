Date Difference

Find the difference between two dates.

How to use

  1. Pick the start and end dates.
  2. Tick "include end date" if both days should count.
  3. Read the difference.

Example

1 January 2026 to 31 March 2026 is 2 months 30 days, or 89 days (90 if you include the end date).

Helpful information

What are weekdays?

Monday to Friday. Bangladesh weekends are Friday and Saturday, and public holidays are not subtracted, so treat this as a guide for working-day counts.
