About HisabCulator

HisabCulator is a simple online calculation platform designed to make everyday calculations easier. It has calculators, converters, education tools, fitness tools and date tools, each on its own page.

Why we built it

Many calculator sites are cluttered, ask you to sign up, or ignore how things work in Bangladesh. HisabCulator focuses on:

  • Students: GPA, CGPA and SSC/HSC results
  • Everyday calculations: percentages, discounts, unit conversion
  • Bangladesh users: Taka, Bangla digits, local units
  • Simple, easy-to-use tools with clear explanations

Our goal

To make useful calculations simple, accessible and easy to understand.

Bangladesh-focused

  • Bangla number support (type or view ০১২৩৪৫৬৭৮৯)
  • BDT in the discount calculator and currency converter
  • SSC/HSC GPA with the 5.00 board scale
  • 4th subject handling
  • University CGPA on the 4.00 scale
  • Maund, seer, tola and hat converters

Privacy and simplicity

There are no accounts and no sign-up. Calculations run in your browser. Your theme, language, calculator history and any currency rates you edit are saved in your own browser storage, nowhere else. The only request the site makes is when you press “Fetch latest rates” on the currency page, which asks a public exchange-rate service for current numbers. The contact form opens your own email app; it does not send anything by itself.
