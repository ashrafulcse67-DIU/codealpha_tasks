Percentage Calculator

Calculate percentages quickly and easily.

What is X% of Y?

X is what % of Y?

Percentage change

Increase or decrease by %

How to use

  1. Pick the question that matches yours.
  2. Fill in the two numbers.
  3. The answer appears as you type.

Example

15% of 2,000 is 300. 45 is 18% of 250. Going from 80 to 100 is a 25% increase.

Helpful information

Percentage change vs. percentage points?

Going from 10% to 15% is a 5 percentage point rise, but a 50% increase in the rate itself.

Why is "what percent" an error when the second number is 0?

You cannot divide by zero, so there is no percentage to show.
