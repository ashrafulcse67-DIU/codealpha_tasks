    How to use

    1. Type with your keyboard or tap the keys.
    2. Use brackets and % when you need them. The answer previews as you type.
    3. Press = or Enter to save it to history. Tap a history line to reuse the answer.

    Example

    250 + 15% gives 287.5 (15% of 250 is added). (120 − 20) ÷ 4 gives 25.

    Helpful information

    How does % work?

    Alone, 50% means 0.5. After + or −, 10% means 10% of the number before it, so 200 + 10% is 220.

    Can I type Bangla digits?

    Yes. ১২ + ৩ works. Turn on বাংলা to see answers in Bangla digits.

    Is my history saved?
